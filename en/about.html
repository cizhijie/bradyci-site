Working principle

Clear information before promises

I do not want to create confidence by inventing specifications or presenting every project as the same standard package.

No blind horsepower matching

Nominal horsepower alone does not describe the refrigeration capacity available at the actual evaporating and condensing conditions.

No invented technical data

Specific capacities, models and performance claims should be supported by the relevant manufacturer information and operating condition.

No need to know everything first

Buyers can start with the information they have. Missing project parameters can be identified during the discussion.
