Project process

From cold-room requirement to equipment proposal

You do not need to know every technical parameter before contacting me. Start with the project information available and clarify the rest step by step.

Describe the project

Room dimensions, insulation if known, target temperature, product and project location.

Clarify the load

Daily loading, incoming product temperature, cooling or freezing time, door activity and local ambient conditions.

Match equipment

Organize the required refrigeration duty and match the refrigeration unit and air cooler to the operating condition.

Confirm & quote

Final configuration, manufacturer performance data, commercial terms, packing and delivery are confirmed before order.
